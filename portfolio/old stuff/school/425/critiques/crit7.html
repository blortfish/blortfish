Critique 7

By Daniel Flint

The Kindle

Revolutionary Change

Most people, myself included remember reading books at home in a paper form. They are heavy, you can't really carry that many at once unless you have a bag and even then it can become over bearing at times. There were attempts in the past made to change books into a lighter form that you could carry around with you on a laptop. Ebooks and pdf files have been around for quite a while now. The problem is that you may not be able to read your laptop outside because it is backlit by led's. If you are reading out in the bright sun, it can become near impossible and straining to try and read a book. Also, just looking at an LCD screen for that long will put a strain on your eyes after a while.

The kindle was a game changer in both of these matters. The kindle took the hard work of lugging a lot of books around off of your back and put it in the palm of your hand. It is a very simple device that can store many books and can be carried by a child. The fact that a screen can't be read in sunlight is also gone. E-ink is used in the kindle. This allows a reader to not be confined indoors while trying to read a pdf. The strain is taken off of your eyes greatly as well. No longer are you trying to focus on a glaring screen, but you will read almost exactly as the page of a book would appear. Computerized books may seem scary because of the possible complicated interfacing. Whereas a book is very simple. All you do is turn the page and you are on the next page. I feel that the kindle did a good job of keeping the interfacing simple.
