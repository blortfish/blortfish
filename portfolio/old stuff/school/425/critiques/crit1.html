Organizer Incandescent Desk Lamp


Affordances

  • Upright Cup- affords placing things for organization e.g. pens
  • Knob- affords turning, allows you to turn the light on
  • Flexible Neck- affords bending, allows directing and focusing of light
  • Upright Fins- affords placing/ organizing, allows for placing of index cards or letters
  • Two Compartments- affords organizing various office supplies
  • Rear Cup- affords cup for change or oddly shaped objects

Constraints

  • Cup size only allows a certain number of writing utensils
  • Flexible neck will not bend in all directions and is weak at some points
  • Other compartments and organizing points are restricted by size

Mapping

  • Knob or switch on light is labeled as on-off switch

Feedback

  • Light turns on upon turning rocker switch/ turning knob

Coloring

  • Solid color allows the light to blend into a modern desk well. Light color inside socket reflects more light.

User's Conceptual Model

  1. 1. See the knob on the back of the light can be turned, > assume that is the way it is turned on.
  2. See the rocker switch on the front, > assume that is the way it is turned on.
  3. See the ribbing on the neck of the light, > try to bend the light and see which directions it can turn, how far it can be turned. Would enable the person to bend light towards whatever is on a desk.

Design Critique

The design of this desk lamp allows the user to keep a desk more organized. Functionally it is very simplistic and therefore does not need much mapping. Although there are not any color contrasts, it seems that it is unnecessary due to how simplistic it is.

The ability to bend the light is also very useful. Being able to turn the lamp in whichever direction you like would be nice for reducing glare on a computer monitor or directing light towards a textbook. The large number of organizing cubbies is very useful when trying to organize various office supplies or placing envelopes.

Improvement

Overall the design is really quite clean and easy to understand. Two small points that could be improved on this product are perhaps making the neck of the light stronger and able to bend in any possible direction. The second item is perhaps making the organizing docks removable in order to clean.
