By Daniel Flint

Suitable usability

Pen vs Pencil

Remembering back to elementary and grade school days it seemed that the pen was a forbidden writing utensil that was very desirable amongst young students. I remember waiting for the day I was finally able to use pen on school assignments. It presented an almost milestone event in education. Looking at it from a child's perspective, it was a restriction on us because of a pen's permanent mark. Both items have the same overall purpose. They leave marks on a paper or substance to record ideas, thoughts, and work.

First to discuss the pros and cons of a pencil being that it is where we started on our writing journey. The pencil is adaptable for a number of reasons. First and most important to us at a young age, mistakes. A mistake you make in pencil can be erased and changed! That is really an amazing feat in itself. There are also a number of grips and erasers and accessories you can get with a pencil. An important use for a pencil that holds true to today is the No. 2 pencil's ability to mark bubble sheets. It is crazy how many times we have used these sheets and they still follow us into college today. Standardized tests, quizzes, surveys, even lottery quick picks.

Now to discuss the negatives about pencils. The most obvious of all is that they will eventually be no more. As you sharpen them they get smaller. To accompany that, you must have a sharpener handy in order to use them. They will eventually become unusable and uncomfortable to write with. That brings me to the next point. Some find them uncomfortable in general due to shape. Of course they do come in some different shapes and sizes and you could purchase different grips, but there is a pretty well established norm for pencils. Additionally, erasers will wear down until you reach the metal. They will no longer be useable as pencils. Another con about them is the fact that they are erasable. It is a con because someone could change data if they have poor intent. So, quite in fact the fact that they are erasable is both a pro and a con.

Pens on the other hand are permanent. They will be your friends into adulthood because you can sign your signature knowing that it will not be altered. Any tests that you fill out and any official document must be signed in pen because of the permanence. You won't have to sharpen a pen either, wherever you are you will be able to use it. Another strong point that pens hold against pencils is the fact that they do run out of ink, but you can replace them if you like that pen in particular. They also come in so many shapes and sizes that you will be able to find the one that suits your fingers best. The come in different colors which is convenient for teachers and graders as well as kids who would like to add color to a drawing.

The negatives about pens are similar to pencils, the fact that they are permanent is desirable in some ways but undesirable in others. Some places warrant the irremovable touch, others not so much. You would not try to make a sketch in pen because if you miss a line, it cannot be removed. Another negative about a pen is they are generally more expensive than pencils and some people are bothered with it more than others, but you can lose the cap or break the clip off of them easily. If you get a pen wet, it could also cause it to no longer work. Even worse, a pen could explode leaving a mess that cannot be cleaned.

As we can see these are two very different objects used to serve the same purpose. Each will be used for a particular application, but they both have the same goal in mind.
