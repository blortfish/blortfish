Popular Brands

There are a large number of manufacturers in the paintball world. Among of the popular choices are Tippmann, Empire, Angel, Ego, Spyder, and more. They all make markers, parts, or equipment for the average user to the professional, to the soldier.

Along with recreational consumers, Tippmann has a Military brand that is used in the army. Paintball has been used and still is used to train soldiers in close quarters and building clearing exercises.
